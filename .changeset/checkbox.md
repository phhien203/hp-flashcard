Adds **`Checkbox`** (and its `CheckboxProps` type), a 16px two-state checkbox built on Base UI. Wrap it in a `<label>` with its text to name it and make the whole label clickable. Use `checked` with `onCheckedChange(checked)` to control it, or `defaultChecked` to leave the state to the component. Unchecked it is the card surface with an input-colored border; checked it fills with `primary` and shows a check mark. Hover adds the `shadow-rest` hard shadow, and keyboard focus shows the `ring` color.
