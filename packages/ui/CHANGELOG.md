## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- [#57](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/pull/57) [`57aab3e`](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/commit/57aab3eec6244c63ab230651cc4352929e11cf00) Thanks [@phhien203](https://github.com/phhien203)! - `Flashcard` now shows the teal "Mastered 5/5" badge instead of the progress bar once `progress` reaches 5, on both sides, matching `CardTile` and the Figma Study Mode "Mastered" frame.

- [#53](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/pull/53) [`475c864`](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/commit/475c8640ae89ede382814fa7a63917064c14235f) Thanks [@phhien203](https://github.com/phhien203)! - Toasts can offer an action button. Pass `action: { label, onClick }` to `useToast().add()`, for example a "Show card" button on a "Your filters hide it" notice. Clicking it runs `onClick` and closes the toast. The new `ToastAction` type describes the option.

- [#29](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/pull/29) [`14318de`](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/commit/14318de4d5455e8c05d37d29d508c5ad7aa54e17) Thanks [@phhien203](https://github.com/phhien203)! - **Breaking:** `Button` now follows the design's Button component set.
  
  - **Variants:** `primary` (yellow, the default), `secondary` (white, dark surface in dark mode) and `outline` (border only, the surface shows through). The `default`, `ghost`, `destructive` and `link` variants are removed: use `primary` in place of `default`.
  - **Sizes:** `default` (48px tall pill) and `icon` (48px circle). `xs`, `sm`, `lg`, `icon-xs`, `icon-sm` and `icon-lg` are removed.
  - **States:** a hard `shadow-rest` at rest that grows to `shadow-hover` on hover, a `ring`-colored border and shadow on keyboard focus, and a faded disabled state that ignores clicks.
  - **Icons:** new `leadingIcon` and `trailingIcon` props place an icon before or after the label. `size="icon"` takes the icon as `children` and requires `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, enforced by `ButtonProps`. In development, it also logs a `console.warn` if both are missing or blank (for example `aria-label=""`).

- [#39](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/pull/39) [`3abfa75`](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/commit/3abfa75994e30442398eafee1a7ddf0fc3264f6a) Thanks [@phhien203](https://github.com/phhien203)! - Adds **`CardTile`** (and its `CardTileProps` type), a card in the All Cards grid. Pass `question`, `answer`, `category` and `progress` (0–5, clamped). The tile shows the question, an "Answer:" label with the answer, and a footer with the category Badge, then either the progress bar with its "n/5" count or, at 5/5, the teal "Mastered 5/5" badge. Long questions and answers wrap; a long category is cut short with an ellipsis. A "Card actions" kebab menu offers Edit and Delete, which call `onEdit` and `onDelete`; the tile does nothing else with them. The tile is an `<article>` named by its question, and props such as `className` go on it.

- [#30](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/pull/30) [`ad05b47`](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/commit/ad05b47bdbaed6713f9be2e50810972b7bcb1c43) Thanks [@phhien203](https://github.com/phhien203)! - Adds **`Checkbox`** (and its `CheckboxProps` type), a 16px two-state checkbox built on Base UI. - [#36](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/pull/36) [`9b8a112`](https://github.com/phhien203/hp-flashcard/commit/9b8a11213ccf23f158e0753d20bb1da50e854a7d) Thanks [@phhien203](https://github.com/phhien203)! - Adds **`Dialog`** and **`AlertDialog`**, built on Base UI, with a `Props` type for every part.
  
  - **`Dialog`** (`DialogTrigger`, `DialogContent`, `DialogHeader`, `DialogTitle`, `DialogDescription`, `DialogFooter`, `DialogClose`) is a modal window for forms such as "Edit your card". `DialogContent` is a white (card) surface with a border, `rounded-16` and the `shadow-hover` hard shadow, at most `container-dialog` (600px) wide with a 16px gutter on small screens. It has a close ✕ in the top-right corner: rename it with `closeLabel` (default `"Close"`) or hide it with `showCloseButton={false}`. Escape, a click outside, the ✕ or a `DialogClose` closes it.
  - **`AlertDialog`** (`AlertDialogTrigger`, `AlertDialogContent`, `AlertDialogHeader`, `AlertDialogTitle`, `AlertDialogDescription`, `AlertDialogFooter`, `AlertDialogCancel`, `AlertDialogAction`) is a confirmation such as "Delete this card?". It has no close ✕ and a click outside does not close it: the user picks `AlertDialogCancel` (a `secondary` Button) or `AlertDialogAction` (a `primary` Button), or presses Escape to cancel. Both run their `onClick` and then close. The footer sits below a full-width divider. Pass `variant` to change either button's look.
  - In both, focus moves into the window when it opens and stays there, then returns to the trigger on close. The title names the window and the description becomes its accessible description. Use `open` with `onOpenChange` to control them, or `defaultOpen`.
